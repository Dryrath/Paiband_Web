downloadable file bytes to the artifact workspace. The template therefore uses
clean initials as automatic fallbacks until the corresponding JPG exists.

To insert the exact portraits without touching PHP:
- export/save each supplied photo as JPG,
- name them exactly as above,
- upload/overwrite them inside the theme's /assets/ directory.
The page will automatically switch from initials to the portraits.

Recommended crop: 4:5 portrait, at least 1000px wide.
